# Alert SLA & Escalation Policy

## SLAs
- **Critical**: ACK within 5m, Resolution within 30m.
- **High**: ACK within 15m, Resolution within 2h.
- **Medium**: ACK within 1h, Resolution within 24h.
- **Low**: Best effort.

## Escalation
If SLAs are breached, alerts escalate automatically to Shift Supervisor, then to Plant Manager.
